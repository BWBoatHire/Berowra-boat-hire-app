Maritime NSW Speed Zone

Areas of speed restrictions (in knots) as designated by signs along the NSW waterway.

Maritime NSW Speed Zone data is available in the following formats:

  • csv
  • geoJSON
  • JSON
  • kml
  • shapefile

The dataset also includes an interactive map, which enables simple data querying and provides a visual representation of the locations. Data is refreshed every week.
