Maritime NSW Towing Restriction Area

Areas where towing of persons is prohibited as designated by signs along the NSW waterway.

Maritime NSW Towing Restriction Area data is available in the following formats:

  • csv
  • geoJSON
  • JSON
  • kml
  • shapefile

The dataset also includes an interactive map, which enables simple data querying and provides a visual representation of the locations. Data is refreshed every week.
